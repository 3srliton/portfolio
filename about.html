Biography

Md Liton Ali is a PhD student in Information Science at the University of Arizona, studying how knowledge moves, and how AI is changing that.

His doctoral research centres on the science of science and AI in higher education: how scholarship is published and accessed, how universities and publishers govern generative AI, and how AI tools affect the way students learn and perform. He also serves as a Graduate Teaching Associate for INFO 557: Neural Networks.

Before the PhD, he spent nearly a decade across more than 15 library and information institutions in Bangladesh. At the Islamic University of Technology (IUT) he served as Assistant Cataloguer and later in the International Affairs and External Communication Office, deploying Koha-ILS, redesigning the library's digital presence, managing 100,000+ items and supporting international students.

He holds Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Information Science and Library Management from the University of Dhaka (Masters CGPA 3.97/4.00), where he received the Mohammad Shahidullah Memorial Gold Medal, the Dean's Award of Honour at both degree levels and the Provost Award of Academic Excellence. His policy review of generative AI across academic publishers and universities won the Best Paper Award at IIKM 2025.
