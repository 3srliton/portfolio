Md Liton Ali is an Information Science researcher and PhD student at the University of Arizona, dedicated to modernizing information access and transforming library ecosystems.

Starting from his university days, he has spent nearly a decade working across more than 15 library and information institutions in Bangladesh, bridging traditional information management with digital and AI-driven frameworks.

He built much of this foundation at the Islamic University of Technology (IUT), serving as Assistant Cataloguer and Liaison Officer for the International Affairs Office. There he deployed Koha-ILS, digitized patron onboarding, streamlined cataloging workflows, and supported international students through every stage of their integration.

He holds a Master of Arts in Information Science and Library Management from the University of Dhaka (CGPA 3.97/4.00), graduating with the Mohammad Shahidullah Memorial Gold Medal, the Dean's Award of Honour, and the Provost Award of Excellence. His doctoral research builds on this record, extending into health misinformation and knowledge management. He also continues to lead a zero-budget digital library framework for resource-constrained school and college libraries across Bangladesh, work recognized with the Best Paper Award at IIKM 2025.
