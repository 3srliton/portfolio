Best Paper Award · IIKM 2025
Ongoing project

Toward designing a zero-budget digital library framework for school and college libraries of Bangladesh

Most school and college libraries in Bangladesh lack digital services, including basic library websites, and the primary barrier is insufficient funding.

This ongoing project designs a framework that enables libraries, especially in developing and least-developed countries, to build and maintain digital services at zero cost, using free resources and requiring only minimal technical skill.

  • Problem: no budget for websites, catalogs or e-resources
  • Approach: free platforms and tools assembled into a repeatable framework
  • Who it serves: school and college libraries in resource-constrained settings
  • Recognition: Best Paper Award, IIKM 2025
