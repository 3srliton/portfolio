Research focus

Areas of inquiry

My doctoral research centres on the science of science and AI in higher education, with related work in information behavior, misinformation and digital libraries.

Lead focus

Science of Science

How scholarly knowledge is produced, published and accessed: scholarly communication, publishing policy, webometrics and the shadow libraries people turn to when access is blocked.

3 works01
Lead focus

AI in Higher Education

How generative AI is reshaping universities, publishers and libraries: institutional policy, AI-powered library services, and AI's effect on how students learn and perform.
