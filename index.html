Best Paper · IIKM 2025
Featured project

A zero-budget digital library framework for Bangladesh

Most school and college libraries in Bangladesh lack digital services, including basic library websites, and the primary barrier is funding. This ongoing project designs a framework that lets libraries build and maintain digital services at zero cost, using free resources and only minimal technical skill.

  • Built for school and college libraries in developing and least-developed countries
  • Free tools only, no budget and minimal technical skill required
  • Recognized with the Best Paper Award at IIKM 2025
